# UDYOGBILL RETURN & CREDIT/DEBIT NOTE CALCULATION RULES
**Standard:** Section 34 CGST Act, 2017 & Indian Commercial Law  

1. **NET EFFECTIVE RATE RULE:**
   When goods sold under a promotional scheme or discount are returned:
   \text{ReturnUnitRate} = \frac{\text{OriginalCommercialTaxable}}{\text{PaidQuantity} + \text{FreeQuantity}}
   The merchant must never refund the customer at the pre-scheme undiscounted list price.

2. **GST REVERSAL IN CREDIT NOTES:**
   - CGST and SGST (or IGST) are reversed in exact proportion to the returned taxable value.
   - The original invoice number, date, and reason must be linked to satisfy GSTR-1 Table 9B reporting.

3. **DEBIT NOTES (PURCHASE RETURNS):**
   - Stock must be deducted strictly from the original purchase batch (BatchId).
   - Input Tax Credit (ITC) must be credited back to reverse input tax claims under Section 16(2).
